359|Playing a quest
360|Is there a time limit?
361|This is a solo quest without a countdown. Pause from the menu and continue later.
362|Can I skip a challenge or reveal answers?
363|Solve challenges correctly in order. Hints guide your observations without costing coins. Skipping and marking yourself correct are unavailable.
364|Will reloading lose results or award rewards again?
365|When signed in, successful actions are saved to your account. Reloading restores the saved step. Each challenge awards its reward once.
